## 2. Why: the diagnosis (G1, exploratory, after scoring)

`out/cl1a_anatomy.md` prints, for each family, its highest record ratio inside every W12 window (to +31 days), where
1.00 is the family's quiet record.

1. **On the pre-registered quiet set, the quiet records are set by three artifacts, not by economics**: the first
   years of real-time seasonal adjustment (F1 and F2 records on 1952-01-24 and 1952-02-07), the 1952 steel strike (F5,
   1952-07-30), and the recession's own aftermath (F3 and F4 records on 2021-05-01, when the 12-month look-back of the
   Sahm gap and of the payroll fall still held early 2020). The re-arm of 182 days lets the same aftermath re-open the
   tool (false alarms 1950, 1954–55, 1971, 1975–76, 1983, 2002–03, 2010, 2020–21 across designs).
2. **With those three artifacts removed** (quiet from trough + 730 days; lines from 1953; major strikes + 60 days
   masked), the labor, jobs and production records — and the joint record of the second- and third-largest family
   ratios — are all set in **1967** (F1 1967-08-10, F3 1967-12-25, F4 1967-06-05, F5 1967-07-14; second largest
   1967-08-10). Against the 1967 stall, by 31 days after the peak month, the second-largest family ratio stood at
   0.29 (1948, three families dark), 0.85 (1969), 0.63 (1973), 0.75 (1980), 0.50 (1990), 0.84 (2001), 0.62 (2007) and
   0.80 (2024). Seven of the thirteen onsets, read on these eight families, were milder than 1966–67 at the moment the
   standard requires the call.
3. F6 (equity), F7 (credit) and F8 (the curve) have their records outside 1967: 1987-12-05 (the crash), 2011-10-19
   (the euro-area crisis) and 2023-04-26 (the deepest inversion since 1981, four days before 2024's W12 window opens).

What this means: on these families, any rule that calls those seven onsets by +31 days fires in 1967 (and, through the
curve, in late 2022 to April 2023), unless it uses something recession-specific. Every tool that has met the window on
the record (the live v3.76; 538's C15) did so with recession-specific routes or gates, and the program's blind tests of
such methods (538 gate 5: at best 7 of 9 with 2 false alarms; 547: 8 of 11 in W4 with 25 false-alarm episodes) show
that the routes do not carry forward. CL1-A's diagnosis is the same finding reached from the other side, with no
selection at all.

## 3. Defects found in the build and fixed before this record

- The cloud inbox's as-printed seasonally adjusted unemployment rate of July 1957 carries 1956 months on the old CPS
  definitions; mixed with 1957 months on the new definitions, the Sahm gap read 0.83 in August 1957 (ALFRED's 1960
  vintage restates 1956 0.2–0.7 point higher). F3 now never mixes eras and is dark from 1957-02-28 until the new
  definitions have 15 months (March 1958). `panel/families.py`, `monthly_reading`.
- A dark spell (NaN reading) was being forward-filled over by the previous reading; `daily()` now keeps it dark.
- The engine's real-time Sahm gap matches FRED's SAHMREALTIME in 786 of 799 months (two decimals); the 13 differences are
  December months (the January seasonal revision), 1963-01 and 2021-03, and a calendar-month rule for the October 2025
  survey that was never collected (`out/sahm_realtime_cl1.csv`).
